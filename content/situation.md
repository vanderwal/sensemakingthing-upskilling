## The situation
Providing an easy means to use a dependable and a reliable resource of evidence-based understanding supports the comfort and confidence in civic participation and voting.

### Why this is open, not bounded
The broad set of information resources and conflicting information broadcast and shared from convenient resources can compound efforts to have alternate evidence-based information used.

## The paradox
The effort to create a voting / polling evidence-based resource that is easy to use may not be used for the unknown reasons people don't check currently.

_Sharpness self-check (pod-declared): 0/3._

### Who benefits from the status quo
Actor: Those not wanting large voter turn out
Mechanism: Seeding misinformation and fear
